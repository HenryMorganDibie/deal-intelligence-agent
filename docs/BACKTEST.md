# Historical backtest

Walk-forward replay over **2024-01-15 to 2025-06-15**: 1290 companies (1012 US, 278 African) x 18 monthly as-of dates, from a universe of 2240; 130 had no usable price or news history; 820 not yet replayed, joining in the next monthly run. On each date the engine sees only sources published in the previous 30 days, and each prediction is judged on the 90 days that followed. Generated by `python main.py backtest --historical`; raw predictions in `data/historical_backtest.json`.

**Reading the table.** *Precision*: share of labelled predictions followed by a confirming SEC filing, exchange disclosure or credible report (strictly after the as-of date). *Official*: the same, counting only SEC filings and exchange disclosures, which rules out follow-up news about the same story. *Base rate*: share of all company-months followed by that event anyway. *Lift*: precision / base rate, how much better a flag is than picking a company-month at random. *Recall*: share of confirmed events a prediction preceded. *Hit rate*: price moved in the predicted direction net of the market (10 calendar days: first close on or after day 10). Earnings surprises and insider trades are judged on price only.

## Does alpha rank signals?

Confirmable predictions split into thirds by alpha (computed as of each replay date, liquidity held constant). If the alpha weights are useful, precision should rise from the low to the high third.

| Alpha third | Alpha range | Predictions | Precision | Mean absolute 10-day move |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| low | 39.7 to 59.0 | 1365 | 10% | 3.77% (1267) |
| middle | 59.3 to 66.7 | 1357 | 41% | 3.62% (1330) |
| high | 66.8 to 95.2 | 1386 | 34% | 3.9% (1336) |

Rank correlation between alpha and confirmation: 0.19. Verdict: the lowest alpha third confirmed least; the top third did not beat the middle.

## Is there a reliable 10-day return forecast?

Not yet. We evaluated a separate, deliberately simple candidate: the median
market-adjusted return for earlier flags with the same market, signal type and
severity, falling back to broader earlier groups when fewer than 30 examples
were available. The holdout is the last six as-of dates; every forecast uses
only outcomes from earlier dates. Acceptance requires at least 100 holdout
outcomes, at least 5% lower mean absolute error (MAE) than predicting zero, and
a positive 95% date-clustered lower confidence bound. The overall model must
also pass separately in the US and Africa before any forecast is released.

| Holdout | n | Candidate MAE | Zero-return MAE | MAE improvement | Direction accuracy | Passed |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| All markets | 2210 | 4.106% | 4.103% | -0.074% relative | 51.0% | No |
| US | 2155 | 4.112% | 4.110% | -0.059% relative | 51.0% | No |
| Africa | 55 | 3.868% | 3.841% | -0.705% relative | 50.9% | No |

The candidate does not beat the zero-return baseline, and Africa has too few
holdout cases to meet the minimum sample requirement. Therefore the system does
not issue a 10-day return forecast. The existing “measured move after similar
flags” remains descriptive historical context, not an estimate of a future
return. Reproduce the validation with
`python -m src.engines.return_forecast`.

The latest labelled as-of date in this dataset is 2025-06-08, so this evaluation
is also stale for use as a live 2026 forecast. The validator requires labelled
data no more than 180 days old before a model can qualify. New market outcomes
must be collected and evaluated on a later holdout; until then, no return
estimate is advertised as reliable.

## Method and caveats

- Detection in the replay uses SEC filings, NGX disclosures and date-restricted Google News. The live product also reads ~50 African RSS feeds and JSE SENS, which have no searchable history, so live coverage is broader than the replay's.
- Ground truth is limited to what the sources record. An event reported only by outlets below the credibility bar counts as not confirmed, which biases precision down, not up.
- NGX and NSE Kenya have no free per-stock price history, so African hit rates cover JSE names only; NGX/NSE price outcomes accumulate from the daily snapshot going forward.
- M&A is confirmed by completion or by a live deal's regulated milestones (merger proxy, Form 425 business-combination communications, tender offer filings, shareholder or regulatory approval, definitive agreement), since large deals rarely close within the 90-day horizon. Base rates use the same events.
- Static calibration only: adaptive calibration, which learns from outcomes, is off so that later results cannot leak into earlier predictions.
